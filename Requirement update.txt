WMS

Current implementation status
- Unique staff email validation is enforced for login accounts.
- New user creation returns to the user-management list.
- Enquiry and quotation workflows now preserve draft autosave and strict role-based visibility.

Business requirements
- Refer/transfer the scope of work and material list from another project.
- Change materials, scope of work and schedule entries directly in the table form with date entry only where required.
- Keep draft quotation data protected and recover automatically without forcing a resume prompt on the next visit.
- When a section is split across a page break in the quotation output, the displayed lump-sum amount stays with the fragment that contains the larger section body so the value stays visually attached to the correct part of the quotation.
- Rate and amount cells auto-expand to fit larger numeric values while the quotation is being edited.
- Revision requests by the Marketing Manager or Accountant move the quotation to Under Revision for the estimator while preserving the approval trail and internal discussion notifications.
- Before final internal approval, the quotation number remains visible in the register, but the quotation itself remains locked from viewing for the Marketing Executive until internal approval is completed.
- A quotation sent to the estimator before final internal approval must not be counted as a revision number increase and should remain visible on the assigned card count.

Workflow roles

- Marketing Manager
- Estimator

Role functions
- Marketing Executive:
  - Add project enquiry (files: Excel, photos, PDF, CAD, Word)
  - View enquiry and add comments or remarks
  - Update project award / client acceptance of quotation
  - Collect project documents from client
  - View quotation number and status in the register without opening restricted internal approval quotations
- Marketing Manager:
  - View enquiry and add comments or remarks
  - Assign enquiry to estimator
  - Approve quotation in the first approval stage
  - Request revision from estimator before final approval
- Estimator:
  - View enquiry and add comments or remarks
  - Add quotation draft and revise under-revision quotations
  - Add costing and submit quotation for approval
- Document Controller:
  - Submit quotation to client
  - View quotation
  - Verify project documents collected from client
- Admin:
  - Transfer enquiry, quotation and project-related documents while creating projects
- Project Manager:
  - Assign enquiry to estimator
  - View enquiry and add comments or remarks
  - Approve costing
- Accountant:
  - View enquiry and add comments or remarks
  - Approve quotation in the second approval stage
  - Request revision when the quotation is pending second approval
